Project context for the Kinetic / cleanwithphysics.com site. Read at the start of every session.
Brand

Company: Nanobubble Technologies Ltd (SC871237), trading as The Physics of Clean
Product: Kinetic — wall-mounted, mains-pressure water activation platform for professional cleaning
Models: Kinetic S (7 L/min), Kinetic X (15 L/min)
Consumables: NanoPods — externally referred to only as Strip and Shield (never acidic / alkaline / super-alkaline)
Brand colours: navy #273146, accent blue #2874C6, background #171b21
Fonts: Bebas Neue + Outfit (stand-ins for Technica Bold + DIN Next LT Pro)
Wordmark: Technica Bold weight 720, white

Voice

Direct, confident, no hedging.
Short sentences. Active voice.
Avoid: "Executive Summary" as heading, "materially" as hedge, overuse of "across," passive distancing, parallel bullet rhythm with repeated endings, "is required" obligation phrasing, triple negatives, "not just" constructions, em-dash overuse.
Avoid "between water and chemicals" framing — positions Kinetic as a compromise.
All efficacy claims use "up to" language pending independent university validation.

Disclosure rules (apply to all customer / partner-facing content)

Never name SIO, faimstech, or Chris.
No pH values, zeta potential figures, particle counts, stage counts, or internal geometry.
No pathogen names. No "disinfects" claims. No absolute "pump-free" claims.
Display units at shows are described as display units only, not working prototypes.
NanoPod names externally: Strip and Shield only.
No patent claim scope detail beyond "patent pending."
No hard or soft copies of materials to be left with prospects.

Positioning

Mechanism framing: anti-redeposition (load-bearing reference: Zhu et al. 2016, Langmuir).
Complementary to microfibre cleaning systems (Vileda etc.), not competitive.
Physics first, chemistry only where physics can't reach.
